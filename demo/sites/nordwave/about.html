About NordWave Networks

Founded by radio engineers and plant operators, NordWave exists to make industrial wireless as reliable as a cable.

NordWave Networks was founded in 2017 in Oslo by a team of former telecom radio engineers and offshore operations managers. They had seen too many automation projects fail because the network could not keep up. Today NordWave employs 420 people in Oslo, Rotterdam, Munich, Toronto and Chicago, and supports more than 140 industrial sites in 14 countries.

Our mission

We believe every factory, port, mine and utility should have a network that is as dependable as its power supply. Our mission is to give operations teams private wireless they own and control, without the complexity of running a mobile operator.

What makes us different

  • Industrial focus: we only build private networks for operational technology. We are not a consumer phone company and not a generic IT reseller.
  • End-to-end responsibility: radio planning, installation, integration with OT systems and 24/7 operations under one SLA.
  • Open ecosystem: certified with leading AGV, robot, PLC and handheld vendors, and partners with system integrators across Europe and North America.
  • Subscription model: predictable per-site pricing instead of large capital projects.

Who we work with

Our ideal customers are industrial organisations with 1,000 or more employees in the EU, the UK and North America — manufacturers, port and terminal operators, mining companies, utilities and large logistics operators. We work most closely with VP Operations, Plant Directors, Heads of OT, CIOs and Chief Digital Officers.
