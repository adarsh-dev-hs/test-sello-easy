Customer stories

Industrial leaders in Europe and North America run their most critical operations on NordWave.

Fjordhavn Container Terminal, Norway

Fjordhavn handles 1.4 million TEU a year and was converting to remote-controlled cranes when its Wi-Fi mesh became the bottleneck: container stacks blocked signals and crane video froze several times per shift. NordWave deployed NW-Edge with twelve NW-Radio 800 cells on existing light masts in ten weeks.

"We went from daily connectivity incidents to none in a quarter. Crane-move productivity is up 40% and our operators trust the remote stations." — Ingrid Solberg, Terminal Technical Director, Fjordhavn Container Terminal

Elbtal Automotive, Germany

Elbtal Automotive runs 120 AGVs in its body and assembly shops. Wi-Fi roaming caused an average of 35 AGV stops per day, each costing several minutes of line time. With NordWave, AGV stops due to connectivity fell by 98% and the plant re-arranged two lines without new cabling.

"Private 5G turned out to be the enabler for our whole Industry 4.0 roadmap, not just a network upgrade." — Head of OT, Elbtal Automotive

Baltic Forge, Lithuania

Baltic Forge, a steel and forging group with 3,000 employees, needed reliable connectivity for overhead crane control and thermal cameras in an environment full of heat and metal. NordWave's NW-Radio cells, combined with a local 3.7 GHz licence, delivered stable sub-10 ms latency. Avoided downtime is worth EUR 2.3 million per year.

Copper Ridge Mine, Arizona, USA

Copper Ridge operates an open-pit copper mine with 40 autonomous haul trucks. Its previous mesh network lost trucks behind waste dumps. NordWave's relocatable NW-Radio 800 cells and NW-Ops analytics brought network availability to 99.95% and allowed the fleet to grow by 30%.

Great Lakes Power Cooperative, USA

This electric cooperative serves 250,000 members across Michigan and Wisconsin. Using its CBRS licences, NordWave built a private LTE network connecting 300 substations and reclosers, replacing leased lines and saving USD 1.1 million per year.
