Industries we serve

Ports, manufacturing, mining and utilities — anywhere machines move, data matters and downtime is expensive.

Ports and terminals

Container terminals are automating fast: remote-controlled ship-to-shore cranes, automated straddle carriers and drone inspections all need reliable, low-latency wireless across huge, metal-filled yards. Wi-Fi meshes struggle with container stacks that change every hour. NordWave NW-Radio 800 cells mounted on light masts cover entire yards with a handful of radios, and NW-Edge slices separate crane control from CCTV and crew voice. Typical buyers: VP Operations, terminal technical directors and CIOs of port operators planning a new terminal or expansion.

Manufacturing

Automotive, steel, chemicals, paper and food manufacturers are rolling out Industry 4.0 programmes with AGVs, autonomous mobile robots, connected tools and machine vision. These programmes often stall because shop-floor Wi-Fi cannot deliver consistent latency or seamless roaming. NordWave gives plants a deterministic network that integrates with PLCs, MES and OPC UA, and supports flexible production lines that can be re-arranged without re-cabling. Typical buyers: Plant Directors, Heads of OT and Chief Digital Officers.

Mining

Underground and open-pit mines are adopting autonomous haulage, tele-remote loaders, ventilation-on-demand and personnel tracking. Coverage must follow the mine as it grows. NW-Radio cells are rugged, easy to relocate and connect over fibre or wireless backhaul. NordWave networks have kept autonomous trucks moving at more than 99.9% network availability. Typical buyers: VP Operations, mine general managers and heads of automation.

Utilities

Water, power and gas utilities are modernising SCADA, deploying smart meters and equipping field crews with mobile applications. Many now hold their own spectrum — CBRS priority access licences in the US or local licences in Europe — and need a partner to build and operate a private LTE/5G network across pump stations, substations and treatment plants. NordWave replaces leased lines and unreliable public cellular modems with a secure network the utility controls. Typical buyers: CIOs, SCADA/OT managers and VP Operations.

Logistics and warehousing

Fulfilment centres with hundreds of mobile robots and thousands of handheld scanners face constant Wi-Fi dead zones as racking is restocked. NordWave delivers stable coverage in high-bay warehouses and seamless mobility for robots and forklifts.
