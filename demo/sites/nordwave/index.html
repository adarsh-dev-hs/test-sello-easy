Private 5G that keeps heavy industry moving

NordWave Networks builds private 5G and industrial wireless networks for ports, factories, mines and utilities — so cranes, robots, AGVs and sensors stay connected when it matters most.

Industrial operations are becoming software-defined. Automated cranes, autonomous guided vehicles (AGVs), mobile robots, connected workers and thousands of sensors all depend on a network that never blinks. Yet most plants still run on enterprise Wi-Fi that was designed for offices, not for steel structures, moving machines and electromagnetic noise. The result is familiar to every operations leader: AGVs that stop during handover, crane telemetry that drops out, scanners that disconnect and an Industry 4.0 roadmap that stalls at the pilot stage.

NordWave Networks solves this with a complete private 5G platform built for operational technology (OT). We combine our NW-Edge private 5G core, NW-Radio industrial small cells and NW-Ops network analytics into one managed solution that your OT team can run without becoming a mobile operator.

Why industrial companies choose NordWave

Deterministic performance

Latency below 10 ms and 99.999% availability for control traffic, with network slicing that separates AGV, video and sensor traffic so one never disrupts another.

Seamless mobility

Zero-loss handover for vehicles and robots moving across halls, yards and quays at up to 60 km/h — no more AGV stops caused by Wi-Fi roaming.

Coverage with fewer radios

One NW-Radio small cell typically replaces 6–10 Wi-Fi access points, cutting installation work in hazardous and hard-to-reach areas.

OT-grade security

SIM-based device identity, on-premise core and IEC 62443-aligned design. Your data never leaves your site unless you want it to.

Built for the people who run operations

Our customers are VP Operations, Plant Directors, Heads of OT and CIOs at companies with large physical sites — typically 1,000 employees or more across Europe and North America. They come to us when an automation programme, a new terminal or plant expansion, or a fresh private spectrum licence makes connectivity a strategic decision rather than an IT ticket.

NordWave projects usually start with a 12-week pilot in one hall, yard or mine level, with clear success metrics: AGV uptime, telemetry packet loss and the number of connectivity-related production stops. Most customers scale to full-site coverage within a year.
