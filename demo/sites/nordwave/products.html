The NordWave private 5G platform

Three products, one platform: NW-Edge core, NW-Radio small cells and NW-Ops analytics — delivered as a managed subscription per site.

NW-Edge — private 5G core

NW-Edge is a compact, on-premise 5G standalone core that runs on a ruggedised edge server in your plant, port or mine. It manages SIM and eSIM device identities, network slices and quality-of-service policies, and keeps all user-plane traffic local for latency below 10 ms. NW-Edge supports 4G/LTE devices as well, so existing handhelds and modems keep working during migration. A redundant pair delivers 99.999% availability, and an optional edge compute module hosts your vision, safety or digital-twin applications right next to the network.

  • 5G SA and LTE support, 3GPP Release 16 features including URLLC
  • Network slicing for AGVs, video, sensors and voice
  • Integration with Active Directory, SCADA, MES and OPC UA gateways
  • Works with local spectrum licences (3.7–3.8 GHz in EU, CBRS in the US) and shared spectrum

NW-Radio — industrial small cells

NW-Radio small cells are built for harsh environments: IP67 enclosures, -40°C to +60°C operation, vibration resistance and ATEX Zone 2 variants for chemical plants. Each cell covers up to 25,000 m² indoors or 1 km outdoors with line of sight, replacing 6–10 Wi-Fi access points. Cells are powered over Ethernet and can be mounted on light masts, crane structures or hall ceilings.

  • NW-Radio 400 — indoor halls and warehouses
  • NW-Radio 800 — outdoor yards, quays and open-pit mines
  • NW-Radio X — ATEX/hazardous-area variant

NW-Ops — network analytics and operations

NW-Ops gives OT teams a single view of every device, cell and slice. Live dashboards show latency, packet loss and coverage per zone; alerts flag a degraded AGV link before it stops production. NW-Ops correlates network events with production data so the Plant Director can see which line stops were caused by connectivity — and which were not. NordWave's 24/7 network operations centre uses the same tool to support customers under our managed service.
