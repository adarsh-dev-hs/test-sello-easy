Accounts payable on autopilot for mid-market finance teams

LedgerLeaf captures every invoice, matches it to purchase orders and receipts, routes approvals and syncs to your ERP — so your team closes the month faster and never pays a late fee again.

Finance teams at growing companies are stuck in the middle. They process thousands of invoices a month — too many to key by hand, but not enough to justify a six-month enterprise software project. Invoices arrive as PDFs, paper and supplier portals; approvals happen over email; and the AP team spends the last week of every month chasing signatures and reconciling supplier statements. The consequences are real: late-payment penalties, missed early-payment discounts, duplicate payments and a month-end close that drags on for two weeks.

LedgerLeaf is accounts payable automation built specifically for mid-market companies with 200 to 2,000 employees. It goes live in weeks, not months, and connects natively to NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Sage.

What LedgerLeaf does

Invoice capture

AI-powered capture reads invoices from email, scans and EDI with 99% field accuracy — vendor, amounts, tax, PO numbers and line items.

3-way match

Automatic matching of invoices to purchase orders and goods receipts, with configurable tolerances and exception queues.

Approval workflows

Rules-based approvals by entity, department, amount and GL code, with mobile approvals and automatic reminders.

ERP sync

Two-way sync with NetSuite, Dynamics 365 and Sage Intacct: vendors, POs, receipts, bills and payments stay in lockstep.

Outcomes our customers see

  • 80% less manual invoice entry within the first 90 days
  • Month-end close 5 days faster on average
  • Cost per invoice down from about $12 to under $3
  • Zero late-payment penalties for most customers after go-live

Who LedgerLeaf is for

We serve CFOs, Controllers, VPs of Finance and AP Managers at mid-market companies in North America and the UK — especially in wholesale distribution, manufacturing, multi-site healthcare, logistics and retail. Our customers are typically growing quickly: hiring AP staff to keep up with invoice volume, migrating to a new ERP, integrating acquired entities or preparing for investors after a funding round.

Legacy AP tools were built for either very small businesses or global enterprises. LedgerLeaf sits in between: powerful multi-entity support and audit controls, with the simplicity and price point a 10-person finance team needs.
