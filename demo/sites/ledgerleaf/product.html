One platform for the entire AP process

From the moment an invoice arrives to the moment it is paid and posted, LedgerLeaf automates every step — and keeps auditors happy.

1. Invoice capture

Every supplier gets a dedicated LedgerLeaf inbox, and your team can drag in scans or forward PDFs. Our capture engine combines OCR with machine-learning models trained on millions of invoices to extract header fields and line items in seconds, in English, French and Spanish. Duplicate detection flags invoices already received, even when the supplier changes the invoice number format. Suppliers can also submit invoices and check payment status through a free supplier portal, reducing "where is my payment?" calls by 70%.

2. PO and 3-way matching

LedgerLeaf pulls purchase orders and goods receipts from your ERP and matches them to invoice lines automatically. Configurable price and quantity tolerances let clean invoices flow straight through, while exceptions land in a shared queue with all supporting documents attached. Non-PO invoices are coded automatically using learned rules by vendor, so AP clerks review rather than type.

3. Approval workflows

Build approval chains by legal entity, location, department, amount and GL account without IT help. Approvers receive mobile and email notifications and can approve with one click. Escalations and reminders keep invoices moving, and delegation handles holidays. Every decision is time-stamped for a complete audit trail that satisfies SOX and external auditors.

4. ERP sync and payments

Approved bills sync to NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central and Finance, Sage Intacct and Sage 200 in real time, with the correct subsidiary, department, class and tax codes. LedgerLeaf can schedule payments by ACH, BACS, EFT, virtual card or cheque and pass remittance details back to the ERP, so cash forecasts are always accurate.

5. Multi-entity and analytics

Run AP for dozens of legal entities from one workspace with entity-specific approval rules, bank accounts and intercompany coding. The AP dashboard shows invoices in progress, cycle time, cost per invoice, discounts captured, and exceptions by vendor — giving Controllers real-time visibility ahead of month-end close.

Security and compliance

  • SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified
  • Data encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit
  • Single sign-on (SAML), role-based access and segregation of duties
  • Data residency in the US, Canada or the UK
