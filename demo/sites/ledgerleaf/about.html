About LedgerLeaf

We are former controllers and AP managers who got tired of keying invoices. So we built the tool we wanted.

LedgerLeaf was founded in 2019 in Austin, Texas, by Maya Robinson, a former mid-market Controller, and Ethan Park, an engineer who previously built document-AI systems. They saw that mid-market companies were underserved: enterprise AP suites were too expensive and slow to implement, while small-business tools broke down at a few hundred invoices a month or with more than one legal entity.

Today LedgerLeaf has 160 employees in Austin, Toronto and Leeds and serves more than 600 finance teams in the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom. Customers process over 2 million invoices a year through the platform. LedgerLeaf raised a Series B in 2024 led by fintech-focused investors.

Our ideal customers

Companies with 200 to 2,000 employees and between 1,000 and 20,000 invoices per month — especially distributors, manufacturers, multi-site healthcare groups, logistics companies and retailers. We work most closely with CFOs, Controllers, VPs of Finance and AP Managers, often at moments of change: a new finance leader, an ERP migration, an acquisition or a funding round that raises expectations on reporting.

Our values

  • Respect the close. Everything we build should make month-end calmer.
  • Fast time to value. Live in weeks, not quarters.
  • Audit-ready by default. Controls and evidence are built in, not bolted on.
  • Fair pricing. No per-user fees, ever.
