Unlimited users, always

Unlike many AP tools, LedgerLeaf never charges per approver. Department heads, clinic managers, branch managers and executives can all approve invoices from their phones at no additional cost. This is one reason our customers see approval cycle times fall from 9 days to under 2 days.

What is the return on investment?

The average fully loaded cost to process an invoice manually in a mid-market company is $12 to $15. With LedgerLeaf it drops below $3. A company processing 3,000 invoices per month typically saves $350,000 or more per year in processing cost, captures an additional $80,000 in early-payment discounts and eliminates late-payment penalties. Most customers reach payback in under six months. Ask us for our ROI calculator spreadsheet to model your own numbers.

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer a free trial? We offer a 30-day proof of value on your own invoices, with success criteria agreed up front.

Can we start on one entity and add more later? Yes. You can add entities at any time and only pay for the plan tier you need.

Do you support UK and Canadian tax? Yes — VAT, GST/HST and PST are handled natively, as are US sales and use tax fields.

How long is the contract? Annual by default; multi-year agreements include a discount of up to 15%.
