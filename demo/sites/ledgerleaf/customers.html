Distributors, manufacturers, healthcare groups and logistics companies run their AP on LedgerLeaf.

Northgate Supply Co. — wholesale distribution, Ohio

Northgate Supply processes 3,500 vendor invoices per month across four warehouses. Before LedgerLeaf, three AP clerks keyed invoices into NetSuite and approvals took nine days on average. Maple Ridge Clinics — multi-site healthcare, Ontario

Maple Ridge operates 35 outpatient clinics as separate legal entities. Clinic managers approved invoices on paper, and medical-supply vendors regularly charged late fees. LedgerLeaf's multi-entity workflows and mobile approvals cut approval time to 1.5 days and eliminated late-payment penalties within two months.

BlueRiver Logistics — trucking and brokerage, Texas

BlueRiver receives more than 15,000 carrier invoices a month. LedgerLeaf matches them automatically to loads and rate confirmations from its TMS, flags duplicate billing and syncs approved bills to Dynamics 365 Finance. The company now captures early-pay discounts worth $120,000 per year.

Oakmont Packaging — manufacturing, Wisconsin

Oakmont adopted LedgerLeaf during its migration from a legacy ERP to NetSuite OneWorld. LedgerLeaf went live first on the old system, then switched connectors on cutover weekend with no disruption to approvers. 3-way match exceptions dropped by 65%.

"Doing AP automation and the ERP migration together sounded risky. LedgerLeaf made it the easiest part of the project." — CFO, Oakmont Packaging

Thistle & Rose Hospitality — hospitality, United Kingdom

With 28 hotels and restaurants, Thistle & Rose struggled with supplier statement reconciliation in Sage 200. LedgerLeaf's supplier portal and purchase ledger automation reduced month-end reconciliation from a week to a day.
