Native ERP integrations

LedgerLeaf works with the ERP you have today — and the one you are migrating to tomorrow.

Mid-market finance teams rarely stand still. Many of our customers adopt LedgerLeaf in the middle of an ERP migration — moving from QuickBooks, Sage 50 or a legacy on-premise system to NetSuite or Microsoft Dynamics 365. Because LedgerLeaf keeps vendors, approval rules and invoice history in one place, it makes the migration easier: you can switch the ERP connector without retraining approvers or changing supplier processes.

Oracle NetSuite

Our SuiteApp-certified connector syncs vendors, subsidiaries, departments, classes, locations, purchase orders, item receipts, vendor bills and payments in near real time. LedgerLeaf supports NetSuite OneWorld for multi-subsidiary companies, including intercompany allocations and multi-currency. Typical setup time: three days.

Microsoft Dynamics 365

LedgerLeaf integrates with Dynamics 365 Business Central and Dynamics 365 Finance through Microsoft's standard APIs. Vendors, POs, receipts and posted purchase invoices flow automatically, and LedgerLeaf respects your dimensions and posting groups. We are a Microsoft AppSource listed partner.

Sage Intacct and Sage 200

For Sage Intacct customers in the US and Sage 200 customers in the UK, LedgerLeaf provides bi-directional sync of suppliers, purchase orders, GRNs and purchase ledger transactions, including multi-entity Intacct structures common in healthcare and professional services.

Implementation approach

Every LedgerLeaf customer gets a named implementation specialist who has run AP teams before. We map your current approval matrix, import vendor master data, configure matching tolerances and run a parallel month before cutover. For customers mid-way through an ERP migration, we can go live on the old ERP and switch to the new one on your cutover weekend.
